# UFO Hunters example station

`UFO-Hunters.HSCommander` is a receive-only HackRF station for listening around well-known UFO/UAP "hotspots", mainly Skinwalker Ranch (Uintah Basin, Utah) and Area 51 / the Nevada Test and Training Range (Rachel, Nevada). It is regenerated by `make-UFO-Hunters.py`, run from the repo root.

- Radio: HackRF only, RX on and **TX off**. Nothing in this file is meant to be transmitted.
- No pipes are named, so every channel uses the built-in analog demodulator (AM for aircraft, FM for land mobile). A P25 control channel is marked `control` so you can follow it with `modems/commander-demod-p25.rxpipe` if you choose.
- Counts: 7 channel zones and 77 channels. The band plan has 6 zones, 19 bands and 35 segments. The scan list covers the SKW public-safety zone and both A51 zones.
- `WATCH …` channels are waterfall bookmarks. Radar pulses, ADS-B, GNSS, Iridium, telemetry and the hydrogen line carry no voice, so listening in AM or FM only gives you hiss or buzz. Look at them on the waterfall instead.

## Listening rules

Listen to unencrypted traffic only, and never try to decrypt anything. Most NTTR ground traffic is encrypted P25. Military UHF air often uses HAVE QUICK frequency hopping or digital voice, so a quiet published frequency is normal. Laws on what you may monitor, especially from a vehicle, differ by state and country.

## What "1.6 GHz" really is

The *Secret of Skinwalker Ranch* team says it often sees a 1.6 GHz spike on its spectrum analyzer. These are the services that actually live at that frequency:
- GLONASS L1, around 1598–1606 MHz.
- Globalstar uplink, 1610–1618.7 MHz.
- Iridium, 1616–1626.5 MHz. Its handsets and satellites are bursty TDMA, so they look like brief "pulses".
- Inmarsat and other L-band satcom, 1525–1559 and 1626.5–1660.5 MHz.
- GPS L1 at 1575.42 MHz, just below.

**Pulse radars are not at 1.6 GHz.** Long-range air-route radar (ARSR) sits at about 1215–1390 MHz. Airport ASR and NEXRAD weather radar are at about 2.7–3.0 GHz. Secondary radar and transponders use 1030 and 1090 MHz, and DME/TACAN 960–1215 MHz. Near Area 51 the published L-band and S-band entries are CRIIS (1350–1390 MHz) and ACTS (1780–1850 MHz) flight-test telemetry downlinks, which are strong, nearby and real. All of these are within HackRF's range and included as bands and WATCH entries.

## What HackRF cannot see

- X-band radar above 6 GHz, which includes marine and airborne radar at 9.3–9.5 GHz and the 9.45 GHz radar at the Hessdalen station.
- Ku and Ka band.
- VLF, ELF and "natural radio" (sferics and whistlers, below 1 MHz).
- EMF and magnetometer readings, which come from different instruments altogether.

HackRF below about 10 MHz is weak without an upconverter or a good antenna.

## Excluded, and why

- **NTTR P25 talkgroups:** all encrypted (S3).
- **HAVE QUICK channels:** frequency hopping (S2).
- **Groom gate frequencies 310.125 and 314.525:** S1 marks them OLD. The bus channel 856.525 is kept but labelled (OLD).
- **"Sensitive" frequencies:** the Rachel list deliberately leaves some out, and I did not go looking for them.
- **Lincoln Co NV 155.310:** RadioReference subcat 10897 turned out to be Lincoln County, **New Mexico**, so I didn't use its frequencies. 155.310 MHz is used by Lincoln Co NM SO, which matters for Roswell; those frequencies are left out until you look the county up yourself.
- **Hotspot-specific local frequencies outside UT/NV:** not sourced this round, so they aren't in the file.
- **Inmarsat, Globalstar and NEXRAD exact channels:** only band edges are given.

## How to add RadioReference data

Commander has **no RadioReference importer today**. Its only CSV import is the dsd-fme key list. Channels go in through the Channels page, or by editing `make-UFO-Hunters.py` and running it again. Public county and system pages are free to read:

- **Utah:** Uintah Co https://www.radioreference.com/db/browse/ctid/2800, Duchesne Co https://www.radioreference.com/db/browse/ctid/2783, the Utah Communications Authority P25 system (linked from those pages; Roosevelt Hill site is https://www.radioreference.com/db/site/45333) and Utah DPS https://www.radioreference.com/db/aid/1946.
- **Nevada:** Lincoln Co https://www.radioreference.com/db/browse/ctid/1752, Nevada Federal https://www.radioreference.com/db/aid/2321, NTTR https://www.radioreference.com/db/subcat/76469 and the Nevada Shared Radio System https://www.radioreference.com/db/sid/11341. For Nye County, use the RadioReference Nevada state page.

Each county page has a **Download** link, `/db/browse/ctid/<id>/download`, with PDF and CSV files. The CSV, and the RadioReference SOAP web service that scanner software uses, need your own RadioReference login, and usually a Premium subscription. A future "Import RadioReference CSV" feature in Commander could read that file. Talkgroups for the P25 systems (UCA, NSRS) are on the system pages and would go on the Talkgroups page.

Suggested first lookups:
1. UCA P25 talkgroups for Uintah and Duchesne (Skinwalker).
2. The Nevada Shared Radio System Lincoln Co talkgroups (Rachel).
3. Chaves Co NM (Roswell).
4. Presidio Co TX (Marfa).
