# Natural Earth coastlines

`crates/commander/data/coastline-110m.txt` is derived from Natural Earth
1:110m Coastline (`geojson/ne_110m_coastline.geojson` in
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector, commit 693f114, 2020-12-13),
reduced to lon,lat pairs at two decimals. It is compiled into the binary.
